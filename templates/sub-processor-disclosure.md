> **Not legal advice.** A template for the public page where you list the third parties that process personal data on your behalf. Fill in the brackets, keep it truthful, and keep it current. Take qualified advice on your specific obligations.

---

## Why publish this

If you use other services to run yours (hosting, email, analytics, payments), those services often process personal data on your behalf. They are your **sub-processors**. Publishing an honest, current list does three things:

1. It lets your customers see who touches their data, which many DPAs require you to disclose.
2. It gives you a fixed place to give **notice before you change the mix**, so customers can object.
3. It keeps you honest with yourself: a public list is hard to let drift.

The rest of this file is the page you publish. Adapt the wording, keep the structure.

### How we choose sub-processors

Before we add a sub-processor we check that:

- there is a written contract requiring them to protect personal data to a standard at least as high as ours;
- the hosting region is one we are content with, and any transfer outside the UK is covered by an appropriate mechanism (for example UK adequacy or a UK IDTA);
- they have a track record we can verify.
