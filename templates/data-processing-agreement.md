**This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA")** is entered into between:

- **[CONTROLLER LEGAL NAME]**, a company registered in [COUNTRY] under number [COMPANY NUMBER], whose registered office is at [CONTROLLER ADDRESS] (the **"Controller"**); and
- **[PROCESSOR LEGAL NAME]**, a company registered in [COUNTRY] under number [COMPANY NUMBER], whose registered office is at [PROCESSOR ADDRESS] (the **"Processor"**),

each a **"Party"** and together the **"Parties"**.

**Effective date:** [DATE], or the date the Processor first processes personal data on the Controller's behalf under the main agreement, whichever is earlier.

## 1. Background and interpretation

1.1 The Processor provides [DESCRIBE YOUR SERVICE, e.g. "a hosted booking platform"] to the Controller under a separate agreement (the **"Main Agreement"**). In providing that service the Processor processes personal data on the Controller's behalf. This DPA sets out the terms on which it does so, as required by Article 28 of the UK GDPR.

1.2 In this DPA:

- **"UK GDPR"** means the retained EU General Data Protection Regulation as it forms part of the law of England and Wales, Scotland, and Northern Ireland, together with the **Data Protection Act 2018**.
- **"controller"**, **"processor"**, **"data subject"**, **"personal data"**, **"processing"**, **"personal data breach"**, and **"supervisory authority"** have the meanings given in the UK GDPR.
- **"personal data"** here means only the personal data the Processor processes on the Controller's behalf under the Main Agreement, described in **Schedule 1**.
- **"sub-processor"** means any third party engaged by the Processor to process personal data on the Controller's behalf.
- **"applicable data protection law"** means the UK GDPR and any other data protection law that applies to the processing.

1.3 If this DPA and the Main Agreement conflict on a data protection matter, this DPA prevails. On all other matters the Main Agreement prevails.

## 2. Roles of the Parties

2.1 For the personal data described in Schedule 1, the Controller is the **controller** and the Processor is the **processor**.

2.2 The Processor processes personal data only for the purpose of providing the service and only as this DPA and the Controller's instructions allow. The Processor does not sell personal data and does not use it for its own purposes.

## 3. The Processor's obligations

The Processor shall:

3.1 **Process only on instructions.** Process the personal data only on the Controller's documented instructions, including on international transfers, unless required to do otherwise by law. The Main Agreement, this DPA, and the Controller's ordinary use of the service are documented instructions. If the Processor is required by law to process beyond those instructions, it will tell the Controller first, unless the law forbids it.

3.2 **Flag unlawful instructions.** Tell the Controller promptly if, in the Processor's opinion, an instruction infringes applicable data protection law. The Processor is not obliged to give legal advice.

3.3 **Confidentiality.** Ensure that anyone authorised to process the personal data is under an appropriate duty of confidentiality, whether by contract or by a statutory duty.

3.4 **Security.** Put in place appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect the personal data, taking into account the state of the art, the costs, and the risk to data subjects (Article 32 UK GDPR). The measures in place at the effective date are described in **Schedule 3**. The Processor may update them provided the level of protection is not reduced.

3.5 **Sub-processors.** Only engage a sub-processor as clause 4 allows.

3.6 **Assist with data subject rights.** Taking into account the nature of the processing, help the Controller respond to requests from data subjects exercising their rights under Chapter III of the UK GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection), by appropriate technical and organisational measures, as far as this is possible.

3.7 **Assist with the Controller's own duties.** Help the Controller meet its obligations under Articles 32 to 36 (security, breach notification, and data protection impact assessments), taking into account the nature of the processing and the information available to the Processor.

3.8 **Report breaches.** Notify the Controller without undue delay, and in any event within **[NUMBER, e.g. 48]** hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting the personal data. The notice will include, as far as known, the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of data subjects and records affected, the likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed. The Processor will provide further information as it becomes available.

3.9 **Return or delete.** At the Controller's choice, delete or return all the personal data at the end of the provision of the service, and delete existing copies, unless the law requires the Processor to keep them. The Processor will confirm in writing once this is done. Routine backups will be overwritten on the Processor's ordinary backup cycle, described in Schedule 3.

3.10 **Demonstrate compliance and allow audits.** Make available to the Controller the information reasonably needed to show that the Processor meets its Article 28 obligations, and allow for and contribute to audits, including inspections, conducted by the Controller or an auditor it authorises. The Parties agree the audit arrangements in clause 6.

3.11 **Records.** Keep a record of the categories of processing carried out on the Controller's behalf, as required by Article 30(2) UK GDPR.

## 4. Sub-processors

4.1 The Controller gives the Processor **general authorisation** to engage sub-processors, subject to this clause. The sub-processors in place at the effective date are listed in **Schedule 2**, and are published at **[YOUR PUBLIC SUB-PROCESSOR PAGE URL]**.

4.2 Before a new sub-processor starts processing the personal data, the Processor will give the Controller at least **[NUMBER, e.g. 30]** days' notice, by [updating the public list at the URL in clause 4.1 / email to the Controller's stated contact]. Within that notice period the Controller may object in writing on reasonable data protection grounds.

4.3 If the Controller objects, the Parties will discuss in good faith. If the concern cannot be resolved, the Controller may terminate the affected part of the service, as its sole remedy for the objection.

4.4 The Processor will impose on each sub-processor, by written contract, data protection obligations that are in substance the same as those in this DPA. The Processor remains fully liable to the Controller for a sub-processor's performance of those obligations.

## 5. International transfers

5.1 The Processor will not transfer the personal data outside the United Kingdom unless clause 5.2 is met.

5.2 Any transfer of personal data to a country outside the UK will be covered by an appropriate transfer mechanism under Article 44 to 49 UK GDPR, such as UK adequacy regulations, or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (**"IDTA"**). Where the IDTA is used, the [`international-transfer-addendum.md`](international-transfer-addendum.md) cover note in this repository sets out the approach and the IDTA is incorporated by reference.

5.3 Schedule 2 records, for each sub-processor, the country in which it processes the personal data and the transfer mechanism relied on where that country is outside the UK.

## 6. Audit

6.1 The Processor will respond to the Controller's reasonable written requests for information needed to confirm the Processor's compliance with this DPA, within a reasonable time.

6.2 Where information alone is not enough, the Controller may audit the Processor's relevant systems and records, on **[NUMBER, e.g. 30]** days' written notice, no more than **once a year** unless a supervisory authority requires more, or unless there has been a personal data breach affecting the Controller's data. Audits take place during business hours, cause as little disruption as possible, and respect the confidentiality and security of the Processor's other customers.

6.3 The Controller bears its own costs of an audit. Each Party bears its own personnel costs.

## 7. Liability and term

7.1 Each Party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations and exclusions of liability in the Main Agreement.

7.2 This DPA takes effect on the effective date and continues while the Processor processes personal data on the Controller's behalf. Clauses that by their nature should survive termination (including 3.9, 3.10, and 7) survive.

## 8. General

8.1 This DPA is governed by the law of **England and Wales** [or amend to Scotland / Northern Ireland], and the courts of that jurisdiction have exclusive jurisdiction, without prejudice to a data subject's or supervisory authority's rights.

8.2 Any change to this DPA must be in writing and signed by both Parties.
