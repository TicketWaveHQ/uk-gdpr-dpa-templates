# Sub-processor change notice

> **Not legal advice.** The short notices you send when your sub-processor mix changes. Adapt the wording; keep the notice window you promised in your DPA and on your public list.

---

## When to send

Send a change notice when you:

- **add** a sub-processor;
- **remove** one and move data elsewhere;
- **change what an existing one does**, or the region it hosts in.

Send it **before** the change goes live, giving at least the notice period you committed to (a 30-day window is a sensible default for small SaaS). For an urgent security-driven change you may act first and notify straight after; say so plainly when you do.

---

## On-page notice (add to the sub-processor list)

> **Upcoming change (effective [DATE]).** From [DATE] we will [add / remove / change] **[Provider name]** for **[purpose]**, hosted in **[region]**. This affects [what data]. If you have a data protection concern, contact us at [PRIVACY EMAIL] before [DATE].

Once the change is live, move it into the change-history table with its effective date.

---

## Email notice (to customers or subscribers)

**Subject:** Change to the services we use to run [YOUR SERVICE NAME]

> Hello [NAME],
>
> We are letting you know in advance about a change to the third-party services we use to run [YOUR SERVICE NAME].
>
> **What is changing:** from **[DATE]** we will [add / remove / change] **[Provider name]**, which will [purpose, e.g. "handle our transactional email"]. It will process [what personal data] and host it in **[region]**. [Where outside the UK: "This transfer is covered by [mechanism]."]
>
> **Why:** [one plain sentence, e.g. "to improve delivery reliability" or "to keep data hosted in the UK".]
>
> **What you need to do:** nothing, unless you have a concern. If you do, reply to this email or contact us at [PRIVACY EMAIL] before **[DATE]**. Our full, current sub-processor list is always at [PUBLIC URL].
>
> Thank you,
> [YOUR NAME], [YOUR SERVICE NAME]

---

## Internal checklist before you press send

- [ ] Written contract with the new sub-processor is in place, with data protection terms at least as strong as your own.
- [ ] Hosting region recorded, and pinned in configuration, not just "supported".
- [ ] Transfer mechanism confirmed if the region is outside the UK.
- [ ] Schedule 2 of your DPA updated to match.
- [ ] Public list updated with the upcoming-change banner.
- [ ] Notice window started; effective date is at least [N] days out.
- [ ] Change-history entry drafted, ready to move once live.
