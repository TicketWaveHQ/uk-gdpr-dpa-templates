# Security Policy

This repository holds document templates and a small, dependency-free
validation script. Two kinds of problem are worth reporting privately:

- a bug in [`scripts/validate-subprocessors.mjs`](scripts/validate-subprocessors.mjs)
  that lets an incomplete or dishonest sub-processor list pass;
- template text that could lead someone to a materially less secure or less
  compliant position than it claims (for example a clause that appears to
  restrict a transfer but does not).

**Contact:** email [hello@ticketwavehq.com](mailto:hello@ticketwavehq.com)
and say in the subject that it is a security report. TicketWave HQ's
disclosure policy, which covers this repository, is at
<https://ticketwavehq.com/security>.

We will acknowledge your report and say what we intend to do. We do not
publish a response time. Please give us a reasonable window to fix the
issue before you publish it.

Ordinary corrections, such as an out-of-date reference or unclear wording,
are welcome as a normal issue or pull request; see
[`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md).

These templates are not legal advice; see [`DISCLAIMER.md`](DISCLAIMER.md).
